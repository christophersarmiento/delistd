Passphrase

{{if .Passphrase.Protected}}Your mail passwords are protected by a passphrase{{else}}An extra gate on your stored mail passwords{{end}}

Mail passwords are always encrypted for this machine and user. A passphrase also stops someone logged in as you from using them.

Heads-up: with a passphrase, runs with nobody at the keyboard can't unlock your mail password. The OS schedule (Schedule with my OS) is refused, and delistd serve or delistd auto ask once at startup. There is no recovery; if you forget it, re-enter your mail passwords.
